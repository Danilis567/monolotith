# MONOLITH — Archival Glossary

Reference definitions for architectural, surveying, and archival terms used across the MONOLITH index (`001`–`027`).

| Term | Plain Language | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `BÉTON BRUT` | raw poured concrete | Unfinished concrete left exposed after formwork removal, preserving timber grain and tie-rod impressions. |
| `WGS84` | GPS coordinates | World Geodetic System 1984, the standard latitude and longitude coordinate reference used for satellite positioning. |
| `CAT. NO.` | catalog number | Sequential three-digit archival identifier assigned to each surveyed structure in the MONOLITH index. |
| `ELEV./HEIGHT` | measured height | Vertical structural extent in metres measured from the primary ground datum to the highest permanent point. |
| `PLATE` | numbered sheet | Individual measured drawing, photographic study, or structural diagram indexed within an archival sequence. |
| `FOLIO` | archive volume | Full-sheet dossier grouping measured plates, site coordinates, and structural specifications for a single work. |
| `DATUM` | reference level | Fixed baseline elevation or geometric axis from which all vertical heights and structural offsets are measured. |
| `SECTION/ELEVATION` | slice or face drawing | Orthographic projection showing either an interior cut through the structure or an unforeshortened exterior facade. |
| `LOAD PATH` | weight route | Continuous structural trajectory transferring gravity and lateral forces from roof and slabs down into the foundations. |
| `PENDENTIVE` | dome-bearing corner | Curved spherical triangle transitioning a square structural bay to support a circular dome above. |
| `FLYING BUTTRESS` | exterior stone brace | Inclined masonry arch spanning outside the main wall to carry lateral vault thrust to an outer pier. |
| `CANTILEVER` | one-sided overhang | Rigid horizontal beam or slab anchored at one end while projecting unsupported into open space. |
